HOW CONTENT ADDRESSING WORKS
SHA-256 maps any input — a 10MB evidence archive or a single byte — to a fixed 64-character hex string. Same input always = same output. Change one character in the evidence and you get a completely different hash. The hash is the evidence's permanent identity.
