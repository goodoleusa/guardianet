GuardianNet today is an app. The evolution makes it an operating system for democratic organizing — a federated stack of open-source tools, cheap reproducible hardware, and community protocols that works when the internet is up, works differently when it's unreliable, and still works when it's gone.

The metric for success is not daily active users. It is: how many chapters could operate indefinitely if guardianet.com disappeared tomorrow?

That is the answer to censorship. Not a better app. A protocol.
